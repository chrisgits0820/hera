HERA — NFL Live Game Tracker


Hera is a PySide6 desktop application that tracks NFL games live. It has four tabs: Game Tracker, Bet Entry, Active Legs, and Archive.


File locations:


* Script: /Users/chris/Hera\Script\hera.py
* Data/DB: /Users/chris/Hera\Data\hera.db
* Font: /Users/chris/Hera\BebasNeue-Regular.ttf
* Team logos: /Users/chris/Hera\Script\ — 32 PNG files named {full-team-name}-logo.png (e.g. kansas-city-chiefs-logo.png) — already transparent, do not process them
* Loading screen: /Users/chris/Hera\hera_loading_screen.png


Design system:


* Font: Bebas Neue throughout — every single element
* Background: #1a1a1a
* Card background: #222222
* Border: #333333
* Green accent: #1cbe1c
* Red: #e24b4a
* Gold: #f0c040
* Text: #e0e0e0


All team colors come from the hex codes CSV in the project — background hex and font hex per team


The app must be fully responsive — all content scales with window size including text and table, no fixed-width layouts

GAME TRACKER TAB — top to bottom:


Section 1 — Game selector: Small dropdown (not full width) on the left showing game matchup and time. Green TRACK button immediately to the right. Dark background row


Section 2 — Score header:
Two team panels side by side with a center panel between them.


* AWAY panel (left): logo top-left → team name below logo → record below name → football icon if on offense. Score large on the RIGHT side of the panel. Panel background = team's background hex. Team name and record = team's font hex.
* CENTER panel: #1a1a1a background. "● LIVE" in green. Clock in large white. Quarter below. Down & distance below that.
* HOME panel (right): Score large on the LEFT side. Logo top-right → team name below logo → record below name. Panel background = team's background hex. Team name and record = team's font hex.


Section 3 — Boxscore:
¾ width table, centered. Columns: TEAM | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | TOTAL. Team cell shows logo + abbreviation on team color background. All text white.


Section 4 — Win probability bar:
Full-width thin bar split by team colors. Percentage labels on each end in team colors. "WIN PROBABILITY" label centered in dim text above the bar.


Section 5 — Active Bets panel:
* Header: "MY ACTIVE BETS — {AWAY}@{HOME}" left, parlay info right.
* Columns: TEAM (logo + abbr on team color) | PLAYER | PROP | O/U | LINE | CURRENT | NEEDS | STATUS
* Default text: white
* Won leg: entire row text green
* Lost leg: entire row text red


Section 6 — Stat boxes (side by side):
Away team left, home team right. Each has three sections:


PASSING header bar in team colors with logo on right (away) or left (home). Columns: # | POS | 


PLAYER | C/ATT | YDS | TD | INT | SACKS. TOTALS row at bottom.


RUSHING. Columns: # | POS | PLAYER | CAR | YDS | YPC | TD | LONG. TOTALS row.


RECEIVING. Columns: # | POS | PLAYER | TAR | REC | YDS | YPR | TD | LONG. TOTALS row.


All stat text white.


POS shown as color-coded badge:
* QB= #9900ff background, #ffffff font
* WR= #ff0000 background, #ffffff font
* RB= #ff9900 background, #000000 font
* TE= #ffff00 background, #000000 font


Section 7 — Play by play:
Fixed strip at bottom. Filter tabs: ALL PLAYS | SCORING PLAYS | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4. Active filter = green filled. Each row: timestamp left | play description white text. If the play involves a player the user has an active bet on, highlight that entire row with a dark green background and keep text white.


Data source: ESPN unofficial API — all live. Polls every 5 seconds for new plays. If a new play is detected, refresh scores, stats, and active bets.


BET ENTRY TAB:


Header: "ACTIVE PARLAY" green left, P1-P10 dropdown + "+ NEW PARLAY" button right


Left panel — BET INFO: BOOK dropdown (FanDuel default), STAKE input, BOOST % input


Right panel — CALCULATIONS: LEGS count, PARLAY ODDS, BOOSTED ODDS, STAKE, TO WIN, PAYOUT (large green)


Leg table below: GAME | TEAM | PLAYER | O/U | LINE | MARKET | ODDS | delete button
"NO LEGS — CLICK + ADD LEG" placeholder when empty


Bottom buttons: + ADD LEG (left), NEW PARLAY (center), SUBMIT PARLAY (right, green)


Odds calculated from American odds input. Boost applied as percentage on top.


All dropdowns populate from live ESPN data (games, teams, players)
Markets list: ML, SPREAD, TOT, ATD, PS YDS, PS TD, COMP, PS ATT, INT, LNG PS, REC YDS, REC, TAR, LNG REC, T+A, SCK, INT REC, K PTS, EPM, FGM, 1TD, LTD, 1H SPD, 1H ML, 1H TOT, 2H SPD, 2H ML, 2H TOT, 1Q SPD, 1Q ML, 1Q TOT, 2Q SPD, 2Q ML, 2Q TOT, 3Q SPD, 3Q ML, 3Q TOT, 4Q SPD, 4Q ML, 4Q TOT, TM TOT
Books list: FANDUEL, DRAFTKINGS, CAESARS, BET MGM, BET365, BALLY, BETR, COURTSIDE, DABBLE, DDC, FANATICS, HARD ROCK, ONYX, PICK 6, POLYMARKET, SCORE, SLEEPER, UNDERDOG, OTHER


ACTIVE LEGS TAB:


Refreshes every 30 seconds
Each parlay gets a full-width header row: ID | LEGS | STATUS badge | BOOK | ODDS | BOOST | WAGER | TO WIN | ARCHIVE button


Below each header, leg rows with columns: GAME | TEAM | PLAYER | O/U | LINE | MARKET | LIVE STAT | QUARTER | SCORE | STATUS


Row colors: green background = tracking live, gold text = WON, red text = LOSS, default = white text


ARCHIVE TAB:


Table of settled parlays: PARLAY | BOOK | STAKE | BOOST | PARLAY ODDS | PAYOUT | DATE


DATABASE: SQLite at /Users/chris/Hera\Data\hera.db
Tables: parlays and legs
The legs table column for leg status is called leg_status — not status.
